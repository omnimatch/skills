# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web report produced by a local agent skill

## Users

People using their own coding or file-capable agents to research potential customers. They need to see the complete evidence-ranked collection and understand which prospects deserve attention.

## Product Purpose

Prospector, Job Scout, Recruiter and Day Tripper are free Omnimatch skills, demonstrating Omnimatch's research method as part of its consultancy offering. Each works without an Omnimatch account or databases and uses the person's own provider keys.

## Operating Context

Guided first-use setup, local research execution, saved checkpoints and an interactive radar report. Prefer a host's supported interactive artifact surface; preserve an openable offline HTML file for every run. Grok Bot gets a companion setup recipe.

## Brand Commitments

Omnimatch's existing dark navy research surface, lilac comparison radar and browsable result list are the visual reference requested by the user. Plain concise language, visible evidence, preserved unknowns, keyboard access and reduced motion.

## Constraints

No hosted Omnimatch service, accounts or compulsory telemetry. No credentials in output. Demonstration companies and people are explicitly fictional. Search and evaluator usage is billed to the user's provider accounts.
